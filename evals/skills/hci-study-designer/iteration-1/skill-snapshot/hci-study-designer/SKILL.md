# HCI Study Designer

Use this skill after the novelty gate is passed and the project is ready to turn into a concrete study.

## Workflow

1. Restate the research question and the evidence needed to support the claim.
2. Choose the study type based on the claim:
   - attitudes, needs, or self-report only: survey or interview
   - system effectiveness or usability: prototype evaluation
   - process or behavior over time: prototype plus logging
   - playful or experiential interaction: gameplay study plus post-play questionnaire
3. Specify participants, recruitment channel, inclusion criteria, and stop criteria.
4. Draft the full instrument set:
   - consent
   - screener
   - attention checks
   - main measures
   - open-ended questions
   - demographics
   - debrief
5. If a prototype is involved, define tasks, completion markers, and logged events.
6. Require a pilot before the full launch.
7. End with an analysis-ready study spec.

## Rules

- Do not design a study before the novelty gate is complete.
- Every online study must include consent and data handling notes.
- Every production study must define exclusion rules before full collection begins.
- If the study depends on a custom prototype, specify the minimum viable prototype rather than an aspirational full system.

## Outputs

Produce these artifacts whenever possible:

- `study spec`
- survey draft
- consent draft
- task script
- pilot checklist
- measurement plan
