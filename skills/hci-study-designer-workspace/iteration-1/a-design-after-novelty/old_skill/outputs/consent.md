## Purpose of the Study

You are invited to participate in a research study examining whether large language model (LLM) assisted tools affect the efficiency and quality of literature reviews conducted by HCI students. This study is conducted by researchers at [Institution].

The goal is to understand how LLM tools compare to standard academic database search when students search for relevant research papers on a given topic.

---

## What You Will Be Asked to Do

If you agree to participate, you will:

1. Complete a brief eligibility screener (approximately 5 minutes).
2. Attend a single study session lasting approximately **90 minutes** (in-person or synchronous video call).
3. Receive a short tutorial on the assigned LLM tool or database search interface (approximately 10 minutes).
4. Complete **two literature review tasks** (30 minutes each), one using an LLM-assisted tool and one using standard unaided search. The order of conditions will be randomly assigned.
5. Complete a workload questionnaire after each task (approximately 5 minutes each).
6. Complete a post-study survey (approximately 8 minutes).
7. Participate in a brief debrief discussion (approximately 7 minutes).

You may be asked to think aloud during the tasks. With your permission, your screen and voice may be recorded.

---

## Voluntary Participation

Your participation is entirely **voluntary**. You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, without penalty or loss of any benefit to which you are otherwise entitled. If you withdraw before completing the session, you will still receive the participation compensation for the portion completed.

---

## Risks and Discomforts

The risks associated with this study are minimal. You may experience mild frustration during the literature review tasks. If at any point you feel distressed, you may stop the task immediately and inform the facilitator.

There is a small risk associated with screen and audio recording. These risks are mitigated by the data security measures described below.

---

## Benefits

There are no direct benefits to you from participating. Findings from this study may contribute to understanding how LLM tools can be designed to better support academic work, which may benefit the HCI research community.

---

## Compensation

You will receive **$15 in gift card compensation** (or equivalent course credit, if arranged through your course instructor) for completing the study session. Compensation will be provided regardless of whether you withdraw after beginning the session.

---

## Confidentiality and Data Handling

Your responses and any recordings will be kept **strictly confidential** and stored securely.

- You will be assigned an anonymous participant ID code. Your name will not be linked to any data in publications or presentations.
- The mapping between your name and your participant ID will be stored separately from the study data and accessible only to the research team.
- Screen and audio recordings will be deleted after transcription and coding are verified by the research team.
- All data will be stored on encrypted institutional servers and retained for up to 3 years per institutional policy, after which they will be securely deleted.
- Your data will not be shared with any third parties outside the research team.
- Anonymized, aggregate findings may be published in academic journals or presented at conferences.
