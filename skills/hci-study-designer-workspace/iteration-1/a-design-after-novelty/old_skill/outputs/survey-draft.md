## Scoring and Analysis Notes

- NASA-TLX raw subscale scores are averaged (no weighting) per Hart & Staveland (1988) instructions; report mean and SD per condition.
- TAM scales (PU, PEU): average the 5 items each; report Cronbach's alpha; target α ≥ 0.70.
- Trust scale (TR): report item-level descriptives; TR2 serves as a behavioral proxy.
- Attention check failures: flag participants with ≥1 failed AC for sensitivity analysis exclusion.
- Open-ended responses: analyzed via thematic analysis (2 coders, consensus coding).
