## 1. Purpose of the Study

This study examines how literature review tools — including LLM-based (AI) tools and conventional academic search tools — affect the efficiency and experience of literature review tasks among HCI graduate students. Results will contribute to understanding how AI tools can or cannot support academic research workflows.

## 3. What You Will Be Asked to Do

If you qualify and agree to participate, you will:

1. Complete an online screening questionnaire (5 min) to confirm eligibility.
2. Attend **two remote video sessions**, each approximately 50 minutes, conducted via Zoom. Sessions must be scheduled at least 48 hours apart and completed within 2 weeks.
3. In each session, complete a **timed literature review task** using one of two search approaches (one session with an LLM-based tool, one session using Google Scholar only). The order is randomly assigned.
4. Complete short questionnaires after each task and after your second session.
5. Allow your **screen to be recorded** during the task portions of each session. Audio is not required to be on, but think-aloud narration is encouraged.

Total time commitment: approximately **2 hours** across both sessions plus 10–15 minutes of questionnaire time.

## 4. Audio/Video and Screen Recording

Your screen will be recorded during both task sessions. These recordings capture your on-screen activity (browser tabs, search queries, document editing) for the purpose of analyzing your search and synthesis process. Your face or voice will not be recorded unless you choose to enable your microphone for think-aloud narration.

**You will be reminded before each task begins that recording is active.** You may pause or stop the recording at any time — doing so will not penalize you, though it may affect what data we can analyze from your session.

## 5. Risks

Risks are minimal. The main foreseeable risks are:

- **Mild frustration or fatigue** from time-limited tasks. You may stop at any time.
- **Privacy risk from screen recording.** Recordings may inadvertently capture content visible in open browser tabs unrelated to the task. We ask that you close personal tabs before starting. Recordings are stored encrypted and reviewed only by trained team members.
- **Data breach risk.** As with any digital data, there is a small risk of unauthorized access despite security measures. We use [institutional encrypted storage] to minimize this risk.

## 6. Benefits

There is no direct benefit to you from participating. You will gain experience completing a structured research task and may learn about LLM-based literature review tools. Results of this study may benefit the broader HCI research community by informing tool design and pedagogy.

## 7. Compensation

You will receive **$20 USD** (or equivalent local currency) per completed session, for a total of **$40 USD** if both sessions are completed. Compensation is provided as an Amazon gift card emailed to you within 3 business days of each session.

If you complete Session 1 but withdraw before Session 2, you will still receive the $20 compensation for Session 1.

Compensation is not contingent on task performance.

## 8. Voluntary Participation and Right to Withdraw

Participation is completely voluntary. You may:

- Decline to answer any question without penalty.
- Stop the task or session at any time without penalty.
- Withdraw from the study entirely at any time during or after sessions.
- Request that your data be deleted from analysis up to **4 weeks after your second session** by contacting the PI at [PI EMAIL]. After this window, data may be de-identified and included in aggregate analysis and it may no longer be possible to remove your specific data.

Withdrawal will not affect your academic standing, relationship with your institution, or compensation for sessions already completed.

## 9. Confidentiality and Data Handling

**What we collect:**
- Screener and survey responses
- Task outputs (your written synthesis documents)
- Screen recordings of task sessions
- Automatically logged task timestamps

**How we protect your data:**
- All data is stored on encrypted institutional servers with access restricted to named research team members.
- Your name and contact information are stored separately from your study data and linked only via a participant ID code.
- Screen recordings are reviewed only by trained research team members under a signed data access agreement.
- Screen recordings are **permanently deleted** after behavioral coding is complete (target: within 6 months of study completion).
- De-identified survey and task data may be retained for up to 5 years per institutional data retention policy.

**What we share:**
- Aggregate, de-identified findings may be published in academic papers, conference proceedings (e.g., ACM CHI), or thesis documents.
- No individual-level data, screen recordings, or personally identifiable information will be published or shared with third parties.
- We will not share your data with your academic supervisor, program director, or any other person affiliated with your institution.

**If a data breach occurs:**
We will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of any unauthorized access to data that could identify you, in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.

## 10. Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools in This Study

This study involves the use of an LLM-based tool (e.g., Elicit, ChatGPT-4o) as an experimental condition. Your interactions with this tool during the study session are subject to the terms of service of that tool. We will not transmit your personal information to the LLM tool provider; task prompts are academic in nature and do not require identifying information.
